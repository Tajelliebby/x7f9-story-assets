ELARION COMPREHENSIVE WORLD GLOSSARY & APPENDIX

1GLOSSARY/APPENDIX

This glossary serves as a guide for the language, customs, financial systems, attire, and cultural terminology of the Therii Empire.

ALPHABETICAL TERMS (A–Z)

A

Adelaide Veridis
The Spring Summoner of Elarion, born to herb traders in the southern marshes of Sundermarsh. She is characterized by her long, wavy golden-blonde hair decorated with blooming flowers, vivid green eyes, sun-warmed tanned skin, and slightly pointed ears that signify her vast magical potential. On her back, she bears intricate tattoos of vines and flowers representing her connection to life and growth. Known for her lively, warm, and optimistic nature, Adelaide is a fiercely protective healer who can summon roots and vines from the earth in combat.
Aedra
Demonic entities that emerged from a cataclysmic tear in reality known as the Rift, seeking to dominate the mortal plane of Elarion, mutate local wildlife, and corrupt the land into barren, desolate wastelands.
Aegis-Wall
see Borderwall
Archons
The four supreme spiritual and military leaders of the Sacred Order of the Seasons, representing Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter. They serve as living saints and divine conduits, wielding elemental command based on faith and acting as the only forces capable of slaying Aedra with single blows.
Ardentis Valhedran III, Emperor
The decisive, pragmatic, and unyielding ruler of the Therii Empire who ascended the throne at age twenty after his father fell in the Rift Wars. He balances the immense administrative and military burdens of the empire with personal relationships, frequently applying tough-love pressure to his cousin, Duke Rurik Iskaldsson, to secure Aurenfell’s future through marriage.
Astrid Vargdottir
The stern, highly protective head maid of Icescar Keep, known as “The Quiet Shield.” Raised in traditional northern ways where order is survival, she ensuring things run smoothly and acts as a supportive, second mother figure to those in the keep, having risen to her post after managing the chaos of the Siege of Icescar.
Aurenfell
The Winter Duchy, a northern mountainous realm of perpetual winter, sacred pine forests, and resilient warrior clans acting as the primary bulwark against the northern Aedra. It is inspired by Tsarist Russia and the Scottish Highlands, governed by the ancient Iskaldsson bloodline under strict honor codes, a Table of Ranks, and unique customs like the Fosterage System. Natively known as Zimajorð.
Aurenfolk
The hardy, honor-bound northern people of Aurenfell, forged from a mixture of original northern brother clans and integrated survivors of historical northern campaigns, heavily relying on ancestral traditions, storytelling, and direct honesty for survival.

B

Betrayers, The
A fanatical, heretical cult of human magic-users who worship the Aedra as the true rulers of the world and view the deity of the One Light as a false god. They wield shadow magic, control minds, and develop a physical corruption marked by a dull grey skin tint spreading from their hands and feet.
Blazing Host, The
The elite offensive military legion of the Summer Archon, characterized by gold-and-crimson armor and fire-etched weaponry, tasked with leading crusades against the Aedra and their heretical followers under the doctrine “Let the light consume what the shadow corrupts.”
Borderwall
The massive, ancient, and rune-etched defensive barrier in Aurenfell separating the duchy from the northern Aedra, constantly maintained and guarded by the duchy’s forces. Each family in Aurenfell is expected to send at least one member to serve as its guardian. Also referred to as the Aegis-Wall.
Breath of Stillness
The divine elemental power of the Winter Archon, capable of halting all movement and sound in a targeted area.
Busby
A high, structured fur hat made of sable or mink, worn by northern nobles during formal outdoor winter processions to maintain body heat in the sub-zero climate.

C

Cecily
Adelaide’s middle maid, characterized by her curly brown hair, warm hazel eyes, and exceptionally empathetic, nurturing nature, making her a comforting favorite among Adelaide’s household staff.
Celestial Library, The
The enormous, labyrinthine archive attached to the Valhyris Spire, housing the empire’s most sacred texts, including scrolls dating back to the founding, and containing highly guarded secrets about the Rift and the origins of the Aedra.
Clara Sigridsson
A slender, youthful, understudy maid in Icescar Keep under Astrid Vargdottir, characterized by golden hair, bright blue eyes, and an endearing, talkative charm. Orphaned during a northern Rift raid as a child, she is secretly a deeply embedded Aedra spy who reports on the Duke’s movements and actively sabotages supply lines.
Compromised
A terrifying Regency-era social state where an unmarried woman is left unchaperoned with a gentleman, instantly destroying her eligibility and reputation in the eyes of the Ton, regardless of whether any impropriety actually occurred.
Coward’s Mark, The
A mark of absolute social disgrace in Aurenfell brought on by deserting a post at the Borderwall or showing cowardice during a winter crisis, legally stripping a family of their land and destroying their name for three generations.
Cut Direct, The
The ultimate social execution in Valestrian high society, performed by looking directly at an acquaintance in public and deliberately pretending they do not exist.

D

Dame Solveig Kaldsdottir
A tall, muscular knight of the Northern Watch from the Highland Greystag Clan who wields twin axes and acts as Adelaide’s fierce protector, holding no patience for noble games or courtly pretense.
Dandy Standard, The
The immaculate Valestren gentlemen’s fashion standard introduced by Mr. Brummell, requiring tailored wool tailcoats cut short in the front, skin-tight breeches, starch-stiffened cravats folding to the chin, and a nipped, wasp-like waist.
Day of Embers
A solemn annual day of remembrance for those lost in the Rift Wars, featuring quiet processions, offerings of black emberstone, the public recitation of the fallen’s names, and the lighting of the Eternal Flame in the capital.
Desertion Sin, The
The ultimate social death in Risenholt, defining the act of fleeing from responsibility—whether leaving a post at the front line or abandoning family duty—resulting in immediate forfeiture of all assets and public disgrace.
Dowry
A substantial sum of money, property, trade shares, or spice stocks provided by a bride’s father to secure a marriage settlement.

E

Eddric Lawson Mirevale, Duke
The robust, Master Trader and Duke of Sundermarsh, characterized by a sun-reddened face, thick mustache, and a booming laugh. Born to a failed merchant, he built his reputation on marsh spices and charisma, frequently settling local disputes with food and overlong stories.
Eisenwacht
see Risenholt
Elaine
Adelaide’s eldest maid, a sharp-featured woman with ice-blue eyes and pale blonde hair braided into a crown, who acts as a calm, serious leader to Adelaide’s maids and a guiding mother figure.
Elemental Guardians
The front-line warrior-priests of the Sacred Order of the Seasons who wield element-based divine authority, conducting blessings, exorcisms, and relic burnings.
Ember Psalms, The
Sacred hymns sung by soldiers and Guardians before battle to invoke the protection of Iltheris and bolster troop morale.
Ember Quarter, The
The northern industrial district of Valestra, filled with smithies, enchanters, and artificers producing enchanted weapons and armor for the war effort against the Aedra.
Empire Silhouette
The elegant, fluid gown silhouette worn by Valestren women, featuring a raised waistline directly under the bust, a low, squared décolletage, and a loose, columnar skirt that pools in a delicate train.

F

Flame of Revelation
The divine elemental power of the Summer Archon, used in inquisitions to burn away falsehood, lies, and shadow corruption.
Fortified Trench Pea Soup
A steaming, incredibly thick purée of dried yellow peas boiled with smoked ham hocks and marrow bones in Risenholt, designed to be dense enough to hold a spoon upright for highway sentries.
Fracturing of Faith, The
The mythic event where the Therii’s pride split the earth, creating the Rift as a divine punishment and releasing the demonic Aedra into the world.
Frostspire Keep
The ancestral ducal seat of Aurenfell perched on a high cliff overlooking Icescar, designed as a robust military fortress with grey stone walls, decorated with wood carvings, tapestries, and trophies from past campaigns.
Frozen Highlands
The rugged, sacred region of Aurenfell featuring snow-capped mountains, glacial waterfalls like Saga Falls, and the Giants’ Spines peaks.

G

Giants’ Spines
The jagged, snow-covered peaks in Aurenfell’s Frozen Highlands that serve as ancestral burial grounds and contain ancient burial cairns.
Great Earthquake, The
The cataclysmic tectonic disaster that shattered the continent of Elarion, destroying the western territories and opening the First Rift in the east.
Guilder
The standard gold currency of Sundermarsh, minted alongside the silver Stiver, and recognized as the most stable, uncorrupted coin on the continent.
Guinea
A premium Valestren unit of account worth 21 shillings, used strictly by high society for elite transactions (such as tailor bills or gambling debts) to signal prestige.

H

Harald Olavsson
A massive, heavily scarred warrior from Aurenfell serving as Duke Rurik’s personal guard and personal protector, nicknamed “The Stone Wall” due to his silent, stoic, and utterly dependable presence in battle. He is Henrik’s older brother.
Henrik Olavsson
A wiry, sandy-blond personal aide and witty jester to Duke Rurik, nicknamed “The Trickster of Bearhook” for his crooked grin and street smarts used to deflect tension with humor. He is Harald’s younger brother.
High Hum, The
Valestrian high-ton slang meaning the absolute pinnacle of high fashion, sophistication, and elite courtly society during the London-style Season.
High Wardens
Regional leaders and priest-commanders of the Sacred Order who oversee fortresses, abbeys, and temples, acting as judges during trials and investigations.

I

Ice Market
A bustling center of commerce in Icescar and Sundermarsh where traders and citizens exchange furs, winter crops, magical wares, rare herbs, and demon parts.
Icescar
The capital city of Aurenfell, a formidable fortress-city nestled in a mountain valley, wrapped in snow, and protected by black stone walls. Natively known as Raven’s Crag.
Iltheris
see One Light, The
Indian Cotton Muslin
An ethereal, gauzy, and semi-transparent fabric that dominates Valestren daywear, famously dampened by ladies to achieve a cool, clinging drape against the skin.
Initiates
Trainees in the Sacred Order of the Seasons who must pass the Trial of the Guardians to become element-wielding warrior-priests.
Iron Crest, The
The ultimate social prestige in Risenholt, held only by families who have sacrificed their eldest son to the Rift Wars for five consecutive generations.
Isgaldr Gate
The rune-carved main ceremonial entrance to Frostspire Keep, warded with ice magic and translating natively as Ice-Song or Ice-Spell Gate.
Iskaldsson
The ancient ruling noble house of Aurenfell, currently led by Duke Rurik Iskaldsson, tied to the mythical Frost Wolf totem.

J

Joachim Haldmirsson
The elderly, stiff-postured chamberlain of Icescar Keep, characterized by a hawkish nose and deep-set eyes, who has served three generations of the Iskaldsson family with meticulous adherence to custom.
Jointure
A legally guaranteed annual income settled on a bride by her groom’s family to protect her from financial disaster in the event of his death.

K

Kadavergehorsam
Blind, corpse-like obedience without tactical reason, a concept heavily criticized in Risenholt’s merit-based, disciplined military corps.
Kaelen Ignis
The Summer Summoner of Elarion, born to a line of Risenholt guardians. He is towering, broad-shouldered, and thick-built, with sun-bronzed skin, shaggy rust-red hair, and amber eyes. His back is marked by charcoal-colored flame tattoos, scorch marks, and Rift campaign scars. Bold, passionate, and competitive, Kaelen is nicknamed “Dravik” in his hometown.
King’s Coin, The
Ancient, heavy silver coins from the pre-cataclysm era hoarded by the northern Boyar clans as a stable safeguard against paper money or new foreign currencies.

L

Lady Maerith Iskaldsdottir
The loving but firm former Duchess of Aurenfell and mother to Duke Rurik, who encourages him to marry and secure an heir. Known in her youth as “The Iron Rose” due to her resilience and sharpness, her words still carry immense weight among the northern nobility.
Lineage Sin, The
Adultery in Aurenfell, prosecuted as high treason because passing strategic clan lands or fortifications to an illegitimate child breaks the entire defensive structure of the North.
Living Vow
The divine elemental power of the Spring Archon, granting control over plant life, healing, and agricultural rejuvenation.
Livre
The standard silver coin used alongside the gold Sovereign in Valestra’s pre-decimal monetary system.
Long Night’s Watch
The winter solstice celebration in Aurenfell, spent sharing ghost stories and legends of fallen heroes around the hearth.

M

Making a Leg
A formal, deep bow executed by Valestren gentlemen by sliding one foot forward and bending elegantly at the waist.
Maris
Adelaide’s youngest maid, a mischievous, copper-haired woman with green eyes and a petite, nervous frame.
Marked, The
Children born with pointed ears, representing potential for immense elemental magic, who must undergo a sacred blessing ritual to confirm their place in the Light.
Market of Winds
A vibrant, bustling market in Valestra where merchant guilds and spell casters trade wares from across the empire.
Marks of Covenant
Sacred, glowing elemental tattoos worn by the Elemental Guardians that grow in complexity as they ascend in rank.
Marriage Settlement
A legally binding contract negotiated by lawyers prior to marriage in Valestra to control the flow of property, dowries, and pin money.
Marshlight Festival
A spectacular autumn celebration in Sundermarsh where merchants and sailors float thousands of lanterns on the canals to symbolize hope and prosperity.
Mr. Vice
The junior officer who presides over Risenholt military mess nights, enforcing strict dining protocol and issuing physical penalties under a ticking clock.

N

Negus
A warming, aristocratic capital beverage of red wine, boiled water, spices, lemon, and calf’s foot jelly, served after late-night assemblies.

O

Oath-Blade Festival
A grueling knightly rite of passage in Aurenfell involving wilderness survival trials, blood oaths, and grand feasting.
One Light, The
The sole creator and divine force worshipped by the Therii people, embodying order, balance, and harmony. It is the source of power for the Archons, who act as conduits between the divine and mortal realms. Natively known as Iltheris.
Order of Elemental Guardians
see Sacred Order of the Seasons
Orgeat
A sweet, milky syrup made of almonds and orange flower water, served as refreshment at capital balls.

P

Pin Money
A pre-negotiated personal allowance for a wife’s personal expenses, established in Valestren marriage settlements.

Q

Quarter Days
The four specific seasonal days of the year when rents, allowances, and taxes are paid in Valestra, often causing severe cash flow crunches for reckless nobles.

R

Radiant Dawn, The
The grand summer festival celebrating the founding of the Order and the divine light of Iltheris, featuring parades, prayers, and lantern releases.
Rations Coins
Specialized copper tokens distributed to soldiers and citizens in Risenholt to buy grain, gunpowder, or fine wine from garrison stores.
Raven’s Crag
see Icescar
Regent’s Punch
The ultimate, intoxicating beverage of the Valestren elite, consisting of citrus peels, cognac, arrack, Jamaican rum, champagne, pineapple syrup, and cold green tea.
Reticule, The
A tiny, elegantly embroidered drawstring pouch worn at the waist by Valestren ladies to carry smelling salts and fans, as sheer dresses lack pockets.
Riding Habit, The
The structured, masculine-tailored uniform worn daily by Risenholt noblewomen, featuring stiff high collars and narrow, heavy dark skirts.
Rift Wars
The continuous military struggle between the unified Therii Empire and the invading forces of the Rift.
Rift, The
The cataclysmic Eastern tear in reality from which the Aedra emerged, establishing a corrupted war zone.
Riftlands
The desolate, eastern expanse of scorched land and volcanic pits corrupted by Aedra influence.
Riftspire Citadel
Positioned at the western edge of Valestra, a towering military stronghold of obsidian stone built after the Great Earthquake.
Rijsttafel
A magnificent, spicy banquet in Sundermarsh consisting of dozens of small sharing plates, heavily seasoned with rare imported peppers, ginger, and nutmeg.
Risenholt
The heavily fortified border outpost of the empire, transformed from a peaceful farming town into a militarized war zone near the Rift’s edge. Natively known as Eisenwacht.
Rouble
The silver currency of Aurenfell, often supplemented in the winter by direct trade of timber, iron ingots, or high-value furs.
Rowena Morghal
The Autumn Summoner of Elarion, born into a legacy of elemental philosophers in Valestra. Elegant and ethereal, she has auburn hair streaked with gold and brown, hazel eyes, and wind/leaf tattoos on her back. Rowena is a wise, introspective, and patient observer who wears flowing robes of burnt orange and gold.
Runeblade Forge
The ritualistic weapon-forging grounds in Aurenfell where enchanted and ceremonial steel is hand-forged.
Rurik Iskaldsson, Duke
The Duke of Aurenfell, sword master, and Guardian of the Northern Border. He is tall and muscular, with pale skin, midnight black hair, and piercing gray eyes. Known as “The Wolf of Iskald,” he is stoic, calculating, and deeply honorable. He has slightly pointed ears that mark his ice affinity and master cold-based combat. Haunted by his father’s legacy, Rurik initially struggles with duty and marriage pressure.

S

Sacred Order of the Seasons
The primary religious and military organization of Elarion, combining spiritual leadership with martial authority to defend the empire, also known as the Seraphim of the Fourfold Light or Order of Elemental Guardians.
Saga Falls
A sacred pilgrimage site in Aurenfell where glacial waters cut through the Highlands, believed to facilitate communion with ancestors.
Scales of Balance, The
A major political and spiritual ritual held every ten years to reinforce the divine barriers of the Riftspire Citadel.
Seagrass Bracelets
Hand-woven grass bracelets exchanged by Sundermarsh lovers in place of metal rings, worn continuously until they naturally fray and fall away.
Seals of the Archon
Literal glowing sigils from Iltheris that affirm the divine status and seasonal elemental authority of the Archons.
Seraphim of the Fourfold Light
see Sacred Order of the Seasons
Shako
A tall, cylindrical military cap adorned with feathers or polished metal plates, worn by Risenholt officers during formal assemblies.
Shield-Oath
A legally binding, spiritual military contract that serves as the northern marriage vow, sworn over drawn blades before the clan.
Sight of the Turning Wheel
The divine elemental power of the Autumn Archon, granting prophetic visions and deep historical wisdom.
Silent Veil, The
The secretive inquisition, intelligence, and heresy containment unit of the Winter Archon, operating in absolute silence during rites under the doctrine “One voice may doom a thousand.”
Soliélis-sur-Zhaen
see Valestra
Sovereign
The standard Valestren gold coin worth 20 shillings, representing the peak pre-decimal currency of the Ton.
Spartan Wash, The
A disciplined grooming ritual in Risenholt consisting of rapid, ice-cold water washes to build character and physical stamina.
Stewards of the Bough, The
The peacekeeping and rural mediation force of the Autumn Archon, tasked with maintaining domestic order under the doctrine “Justice without kindness is tyranny in disguise.”
Stiver
The standard silver coin used alongside the gold Guilder in Sundermarsh.
Summit of Seasons, The
The grand marble monastery and sanctuary in Valestra, home to the Four Great Elemental Summoners.
Sundermarsh
A wealthy, low-lying southern port town and trading hub surrounded by marshlands, governed by a practical merchant oligarchy. It is inspired by the Kingdom of Holland and Spanish Louisiana, emphasizing spice-trade wealth over noble titles. Natively known as Zuyder-Moeras.
Swamp Fire Punch
A highly potent southern punch featuring juniper gin, sugarcane syrup, lime juice, and nutmeg, served at floating docks.

T

Thaler
The standard silver coin of Risenholt, minted alongside the heavy Iron Mark to fuel the war economy.
Therii
The dominant human population of the empire, defined by their strict social castes, devotion to Iltheris, and pointed ears denoting magic potential.
Therii Empire, The
The centralized theocratic empire of Elarion governed from Valestra, encompassing four surviving regional duchies and territories.
Thorne Gelid
The Winter Summoner of the Kingdom. Imposing and tall, with snow-white hair, pale skin, and piercing ice-blue eyes, his back is tattooed with jagged ice crystals and mountains. He is a stoic, quiet, and disciplined leader who governs with unyielding endurance.
Trial of the Guardians
The secluded, grueling test of elemental will and faith that initiates must endure to join the ranks of the warrior-priests.

V

Valestra
The sprawling cultural and political capital city of the empire, characterized by Regency-inspired elegance and Grand-French salons. It bathes in warm climate and sits in a fertile valley surrounded by hills. Natively known as Soliélis-sur-Zhaen.
Valhyris Spire
The grand, impossibly tall imperial palace of Valestra, crafted of white marble and obsidian, built upon the city’s highest hill to symbolize the imperial dynasty’s divine rule.
Vanguard Plaza
The massive public square in Icescar used for martial ceremonies, assemblies, and public thing-like meetings of Aurenfolk.
Vigilhold
A legendary, strategic northern fortress in Aurenfell that has never fallen, acting as a training ground for warriors and a refuge for displaced border populations.
Viscount Elric Eve
A tall, graceful, and immaculately dressed Imperial courtier in navy silks, known for his silver-tongued diplomacy, subtle smile, and polished diplomacy in Valestra’s upper caste.

W

War Bond
State-issued, interest-bearing securities in Risenholt where wealthy families store and accumulate wealth to fund border defenses.
War Widow Pension
A unique Risenholt law guaranteeing military wives full ownership of their husband’s estate and a state stipend if he falls at the Rift.
Wealth Meld, The
A protective Sundermarsh marriage contract ensuring that a merchant’s daughter legally retains sole control over her trade shares and bank vaults.
Wellspring, The
A sacred natural spring in southern Valestra believed to be blessed by the gods, providing pure drinking water to the capital.
White Soup
A silky, rich Valestren aristocratic soup made of boiled veal stock, sweet cream, and finely ground blanched almonds, served hot after midnight assemblies.
Winter Priests
The spiritual leaders of Aurenfell who preserve ancient northern folklore, ice magic, and ancestral traditions.
Winter Tax
A mandatory seasonal tribute paid to the Iskaldsson family to fund the public ice-clearing crews and Borderwall outrider patrols.

Z

Zakuski Table
A spectacular separate cold buffet featured at northern celebrations, stacked with pickles, salted herring, trout, and wild boar cuts to coat the stomach.
Zhaen River
The major, scenic waterway flowing through Valestra, acting as the primary artery for romantic pleasure boats and inland trade.

REGIONAL CULTURAL TERMS & EXPRESSIONS

Aurenfell (Zimajorð)

  • “May the Frost Wolf guide you” — Traditional blessing and farewell.
  • “Blood and snow” — Standard northern binding oath of absolute loyalty and mutual defense.
  • “By the Ice” — Legally binding oath used by northern nobles to seal covenants.
  • “Summer-Child” — Derogatory term used by Aurenfolk for soft, weak Valestrians who cannot handle hardship.
  • “Iron-Spoken” — A term used to describe a person who speaks with blunt, unvarnished honesty without courtly filtering.
  • “The High Hearth” — The place of absolute honor at a dinner table, reserved for a respected guest.

Risenholt (Eisenwacht)

  • “We hold the line” — Common military greeting, farewell, or rallying cry emphasizing collective duty.
  • “Steel and stone” — Common expression denoting stoic determination and resolve.
  • “Iron-True” — Description of a person of absolute, unshakable loyalty and discipline.
  • “The Last Salute” — High-society slang for a soldier who died heroically in battle.

Sundermarsh (Zuyder-Moeras)

  • “Luck be slippery as an eel, but it’s ours to catch” — Proverb regarding seizing commercial and personal opportunities.
  • “Fair winds and flowing gold” — Traditional merchant’s blessing wishing safe travels and rich commercial returns.
  • “To Clearance” — Sundermarsh slang for thoroughly vetting someone’s financial or romantic background.
  • “Deep in the Mud” — Slang for a severe financial or legal mess, or being heavily in debt.
  • “Saffron-Tongued” — Description of a smooth, charming negotiator who can sell anything.

Valestra (Soliélis-sur-Zhaen)

  • “In the Light’s grace” — Formal greeting and blessing.
  • “It would be most improper to assume” — Highly formal, indirect refusal used to navigate courtly etiquette.
  • “By the divine will” — Formal expression of acceptance.
  • “In the Family Way” — The polite, whispered high-society euphemism for a pregnancy.

BATTALION DOCTRINES & MOTTOS

  • The Blazing Host (Summer Archon): “Let the light consume what the shadow corrupts” — Focusing on offensive purges against Aedrean influence.
  • The Silent Veil (Winter Archon): “One voice may doom a thousand” or “In silence, truth survives” — Emphasizing absolute secrecy, espionage containment, and discretion.
  • The Stewards of the Bough (Autumn Archon): “Justice without kindness is tyranny in disguise” or “We gather not to punish, but to preserve” — Highlighting internal peacekeeping and conflict resolution.
  • The Verdant Choir (Spring Archon): “Blood shall not stain hands made to mend” or “To restore one life is to defy the darkness” — Denoting pacifism, healing campaigns, and restoration.
